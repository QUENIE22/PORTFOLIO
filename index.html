About

A product mindset, built the practical way.

I currently work as a Product Specialist at Nexen Innovation Technologies Inc., based here in Oroquieta City, Misamis Occidental. My day-to-day sits at the intersection of understanding what users actually need and translating that into something a team can build.

I have a working, hands-on grasp of coding — enough to prototype ideas, speak the same language as developers, and understand what's realistic to ship. That foundation is what pushed me toward building WasteLess, an expiry management system, and taking it through incubation programs and pitching competitions rather than leaving it as just an idea.

Before product, I spent time on the operations and finance side of things — as an intern at a lending company, and earlier, as a cashier and secretary. That background shows up in how I work now: I care about the numbers behind a decision as much as the idea itself.

Flagship Project

WasteLess — an expiry management system.

Less waste, tracked before it happens.

WasteLess is an expiry management system built to help households and small businesses keep track of products before they go to waste — catching expiry dates early instead of finding out too late. I helped take it from a rough idea through multiple incubation programs and pitching competitions.
